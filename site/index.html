volcasm

the volca sample manager

Manage all 100 sample slots and 10 sequences on your computer, then send them to the volca sample through the headphone output. No extra hardware: one 3.5 mm cable to SYNC IN.

features

 100-slot sample library

Drag in WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG or M4A. Files are converted to the volca format: 31.25 kHz, 16-bit mono.

 Waveform editor

Start and length handles, trim, normalize, pitch, replace, export and undo.

 Memory budget

Live duration and memory meters, measured on the hardware: about 66.8 s, 1019 blocks, 4 MB.

 Sequence editor

10 sequences × 10 parts × 16 steps. Part flags, every parameter on a knob, motion data per step, active steps.

 Preview on the computer

Play a sequence at any tempo from 56 to 240 BPM before you send it. An approximation of the volca's own engine.

 Transfer over audio

KORG's SYRO encoding for selected slots and sequences. 16-bit lossless, or down to 8-bit for about twice the speed. The audio output is checked before anything is sent.

 Sampler

Record from any audio input straight into a slot, with silence trimmed off.

 Factory reset

Restore the KORG factory samples, or erase every sample on the volca.

 Vosyr import

Open Vosyr .vsj projects: samples, sequences, tempo, swing and reverb.

how it works

  1. 81

    Build

    Load samples and build sequences in Volcasm.

  2. 82

    Connect

    Run a 3.5 mm cable from the computer's headphone jack to SYNC IN on the volca. Volume at 100 %.

  3. 83

    Send

    Press SEND. The volca shows the progress on its own display.

The transfer is a loud, harsh noise. Never play it through speakers or headphones.

good to know

Can Volcasm read samples back from the volca?

No. The volca sample cannot send data: SYNC OUT is only a clock pulse. Your project file is the backup, so save it.

Which volca does it support?

Only the first-generation KORG volca sample: 100 slots, 10 sequences, about 4 MB. The volca sample2 is not supported.

The volca shows Err dcod. What now?

Something on the audio path changed the signal. Turn off equalizers, audio "enhancements" and spatial sound, set the volume to 100 %, and set the output to 16-bit, 44.1 kHz if you can. Volcasm checks the output before sending: on Windows it converts the signal to the output's rate itself, on Linux it tells you how to switch PipeWire to 44.1 kHz.

Do I need an interface or a special cable?

No. A plain 3.5 mm stereo or mono cable from the headphone jack to SYNC IN is enough.

Is it safe to listen to the transfer?

No. The signal is a loud, harsh noise. Never route it to speakers or headphones.

Is it related to KORG?

No. Volcasm is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by KORG Inc. It uses the SYRO library that KORG published for this purpose.

inspired by the best

vosyr

By Frederikson Labs · Windows and macOS

The best volca sample manager ever made. Volcasm exists mainly because Vosyr can no longer be downloaded anywhere and never had a Linux version. Volcasm opens Vosyr projects, so your old work comes along.

 Frederikson Labs

volca sampler

By Ben Wiley · open source, MIT

Web-based: record a sample and send it to the volca right from the browser, including on your phone, which Volcasm can't do. Its approach to sample processing and to testing SYRO streams helped shape Volcasm.
